CLP$ 3.000.000/258 Daily rate equals to CLP$11,628. That amount times the day of the number of days of the contract of the employee in the project. If  he started from the very begining up to 31-05-2026 he will  receive full 3 salaries if he he went down let say 28-02-2026 he will receive CLP$3,000,000/258*166=Total Payment CLP$1,930,232										
	2	Production Meters Drillers and Helpers	Total production of the rig where the driller worked during the month (shifts onstite) times the effective days onsite										
	3	Production Meters Support	Total production of the best rig of the month divided by 30 days times the effective days onsite
